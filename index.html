About this project

Objective — design and visualize a custom wastewater treatment equipment system integrating mechanical screening, sedimentation, sludge collection and screw-conveyor handling, from an incoming waste stream through to discharge.

Approach — 3D mechanical modeling, component-level design, structural support design, preliminary strength calculations, conceptual material selection, manufacturing-process planning, assembly visualization and interactive operation simulation.

All flows, dimensions and material call-outs are conceptual design assumptions made for this demonstrator.
